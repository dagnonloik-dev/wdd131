Cotonou, Benin

Cotonou is the largest city and economic center of Benin, a country located in West Africa. It is situated on the coast of the Atlantic Ocean and serves as the country's main port. Cotonou is known for its vibrant culture, bustling markets, and lively nightlife. The city is also home to several important landmarks, including the Dantokpa Market, the Cotonou Cathedral, and the Fondation Zinsou art museum. Despite its economic importance, Cotonou faces challenges such as traffic congestion and inadequate infrastructure. However, it remains a popular destination for tourists and a hub for commerce in Benin.
