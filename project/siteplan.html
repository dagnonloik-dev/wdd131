Site Purpose

Travel Benin is a tourism website designed to promote Benin's most popular destinations and help visitors plan their trips. The website provides information about famous tourist attractions, allows users to search and filter destinations, and includes a trip planning form where users can save their travel information using JavaScript localStorage.

Scenarios

  • What are the most popular tourist destinations to visit in Benin?
  • How can I plan a trip to Benin and save my travel information for later?
